# Tabzoola · Anselmo Die & Index

Tabzoola (tabzoola.com) sells custom printed index tab dividers: binder tabs printed with each buyer's own section titles, priced instantly online with no quote form. Its sister company, Anselmo Die & Index, makes them at its plant in Schaumburg, Illinois, which has die-cut index tabs since 1993.

- Paper index tabs with clear or colored Mylar on every tab, 1 to 150 tabs per set, any cut from 1/2 to 1/15
- 25-set minimum; free PDF proof before anything prints; orders usually ship about 3 business days after proof approval
- Free UPS Ground shipping on orders over $75
- Design and price a set: <https://tabzoola.com/configure/paper> · Price list: <https://tabzoola.com/pricing>
